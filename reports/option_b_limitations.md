# Option B Limitations & Responsible-Use Specification

## 1. Scope & Primary Purpose

The Zimbabwe Commodity Price Stress Classifier (ZCPSC) Option B is an academic data-science decision-support prototype designed to predict next-month relative commodity-price stress:
$$\text{Information at month } T \longrightarrow \text{Price stress at month } T+1$$

## 2. Explicit System Exclusions & Non-Claims

In strict compliance with project governance and ethics, the system must **NOT** be claimed or utilized to:
1. **Predict Household Food Insecurity:** The model estimates district-level market commodity prices relative to national medians. It does not measure household consumption, calorie deficits, coping strategies, or biological nutrition outcomes.
2. **Assign IPC Classification Phases:** The system is not an Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) classifier. IPC phases integrate multifaceted multi-sectoral indicators (FEWS NET, ZimVAC, nutrition surveys) that lie outside this market model.
3. **Estimate Causal Rainfall Impacts:** Feature contributions derived from TreeSHAP or regression coefficients quantify empirical associations fitted by the algorithms; they do not establish causal identification or agronomic production functions.
4. **Forecast Exact Currency Commodity Prices:** The system predicts a binary relative stress indicator ($\text{SPI} > 1.25$); it does not forecast nominal dollar or ZiG price levels.
5. **Substitute Official Assessments:** The tool is not a replacement for official assessments conducted by the Government of Zimbabwe, ZimVAC, WFP VAM, or FEWS NET.

## 3. Data & Operational Limitations

1. **Market Reporting Irregularity:** Remote rural markets occasionally suffer from irregular quote collection. A reporting continuity filter ($\le 2$ months) was instituted to prevent stale lags from distorting inference.
2. **Currency Regime Redenominations:** Zimbabwe experienced multiple currency shifts during the study window (Bond Notes, RTGS, ZWL, and ZiG in April 2024). While the Spatial Price Index isolates within-currency relative distributions, April 2024 ZWL quotes had to be manually excluded.
3. **Thin-Cell Data Sparsity:** Commodities with fewer than 8 reporting districts in a given month were excluded to prevent national reference median volatility.
4. **Explainability Interpretation:** As displayed throughout all reports and dashboard pages:
   > *Feature contribution explains the fitted model prediction; it does not prove causation.*

## 4. Responsible-Use Guidance for Analysts

Predictions generated by this tool must always be triangulated with official agricultural assessments, remote-sensing vegetation health indices (NDVI/VCI), and local market field intelligence.
